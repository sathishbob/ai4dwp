The migration is now delivering a broadly stable experience for most staff, with clear improvement from last week. The early access and login problems have largely settled, file access concerns have eased, and day-to-day connectivity feedback is much more positive. Overall sentiment has shifted from disruption to steady operations, and most teams are working normally on the new setup. In plain terms, the rollout is on track, and the majority of end-user pain points seen in the first week have reduced.

Two issues remain open and are being handled as priority work. First, the floor 3 printing problem is still unresolved and is now affecting confidence because it has continued into another week; this is being escalated with focused ownership and regular progress checkpoints until service is stable. Second, finance users are reporting a new pattern of Excel instability on larger files, alongside slower time-to-ready after recent settings changes in that same user group; we are running fast, controlled checks in a small pilot before wider changes, so we can confirm the fix path quickly and safely. We will continue proactive staff updates on timing and progress, while avoiding promises until fixes are validated.
