# All-Staff Notice: AVD Sign-In Issue

We are aware of an issue this morning affecting some staff who are unable to sign in to AVD or are seeing account lockouts. Our support teams are actively working on it now.

As a temporary step, please close the AVD window completely, wait 30 seconds, and try again using your full work email address and password. If it still fails, lock your keyboard and sign back in to Windows once, then retry AVD.

If you still cannot get in after two tries, please contact the service desk and share the exact time it failed, the message shown on screen, whether you could sign in to Windows normally, and whether this has happened more than once today.

Please stay calm while we work through this. We will send the next update within 60 minutes, even if we are still investigating.
