# Black Screen After Log-In (Self-Help Guide)

If you log in and see a black screen, do not worry. Your files and work are safe.
This is a screen problem only. It does not delete your documents.

## What You Can Do Right Now

1. Wait for up to 30 seconds.
Some log-ins fix themselves.

2. If the screen is still black, log out of your work app.
Close the work app window fully.

3. Wait 1 minute.
This gives the work system time to refresh your link.

4. Log in again once.
If your normal work screen appears, continue working.

5. If it fails again, restart your own computer once.
Then try to log in one more time.

6. If the issue continues, stop trying again and contact the Service Desk.
Too many extra attempts can slow down help desk sorting.

## What Not To Do

- Do not change special settings.
- Do not install or remove apps.
- Do not follow online fixes that ask for special IT permission.

## Information to Include in Your Help Request

Please copy and share these details:

- Your full name
- Your work log-in name
- Best contact number
- Time the problem started (include your local time area, for example UK time)
- The exact message shown on screen (if any)
- What you saw: black screen only, or black screen then gets cut off
- How many times you tried again
- Whether restarting your own computer helped
- Device type (laptop/desktop)
- Your location (office/home) and internet type (office wireless internet, home wireless internet, phone internet sharing)

Contact the Service Desk if the screen stays black after one extra try and one computer restart, or if you are unable to work.
